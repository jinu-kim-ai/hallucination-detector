Apollo 11 lifted off from Kennedy Space Center on July 16, 1969, with Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, and Michael Collins aboard. The lunar module Eagle touched down in the Sea of Tranquility on July 20, 1969, and Armstrong became the first person to walk on the Moon. Collins stayed in lunar orbit in the command module Columbia. The crew brought back about 45 kilograms of lunar samples. The mission ended on July 24, 1969, with a splashdown in the Atlantic Ocean.
