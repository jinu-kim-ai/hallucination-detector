Apollo 11 launched from Kennedy Space Center on July 16, 1969. The crew consisted of Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, and Michael Collins. On July 20, 1969, the lunar module Eagle landed in the Sea of Tranquility. Armstrong became the first person to walk on the Moon, followed by Aldrin about 19 minutes later. Collins remained in lunar orbit aboard the command module Columbia. The astronauts collected about 21.5 kilograms of lunar samples. The mission returned to Earth on July 24, 1969, splashing down in the Pacific Ocean.
